Scholarship FAQs

Many universities and organizations offer scholarships or funding opportunities for eligible international students.

Eligibility depends on the scholarship. Requirements may include academic performance, course, destination and other criteria.

Our advisors can help you explore scholarship opportunities that may be relevant to your academic profile and study plans.

Scholarship coverage varies. Some may cover part of tuition while others may provide larger financial support.

Some scholarships require an application before admission, while others may be available after receiving an offer. Requirements depend on the scholarship provider.

Academic performance is an important factor for many scholarships, but eligibility criteria vary between programs.
